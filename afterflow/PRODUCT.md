## Platform

Web: a scrollable homepage and a compact Insights publication.

## Users

Operators and business leaders at mid-market companies with high-volume workflows and measurable inefficiencies. A buyer should be able to sponsor one focused production deployment. Current audiences are COOs & Operations, Customer Support, Service Operations, Risk & Governance, and Fintech & Banking.

Initial uses include customer support, document handling, case processing and internal knowledge retrieval. Fintech and banking are potential applications of those workflows, not evidence of delivered client work. Investors are a secondary audience for the lasting simulation engine being built through delivery.

## Product Purpose

Afterflow is a simulation company, starting with AI transformation. It models company operations, compares proposed changes, builds the chosen solution with the customer's team and updates the company model from observed results.

The promise is: **Make your company better at getting better.** The continuous narrative is model → simulate → build → learn. One operational problem is the entry point; the persistent business model is the lasting product. Describe autonomous self-improvement as a direction, not a completed capability.

## Operating Context

A conversation and a few examples can start an engagement. The team then agrees the use case, data access, integrations, controls and path to production. Delivery combines a business model, business case and stakeholder materials with working software, rollout and measurement.

“Prototype in a day. Production in weeks.” expresses the focused-workflow proposition. Timing depends on scope, access and approvals; the FAQ commits to agreeing a production timeline for each use case.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Connect fragmented company data, systems and team knowledge in an operational model.
- Test changes against dependencies, capacity, controls, adoption, costs and expected returns.
- Build, test and deploy the selected AI solution with agreed approvals.
- Record predictions before rollout, compare them with outcomes and update the model.
- Retain customer ownership of accumulated knowledge and learnings within their environment. Customer data is never used to train Afterflow without explicit permission.
- Support approved read-only access, record-level permissions and private deployment, including behind the customer's firewall. These are supplied product statements, not certifications.

## Evidence and Claim Boundaries

Apple, Uber, BHP, Atlassian and Mistral represent previous team experience. They are not Afterflow customers, partners or endorsements. No approved customer testimonials or measured customer outcomes have been supplied.

The process diagrams use +32% projected gain and +32% forecast versus +28% observed improvement as conceptual scenario values. They are not benchmark results or live product screenshots. Preserve concise accessible descriptions without adding verbose hover copy.

The approved FAQ says Afterflow achieved nearly **3× the causal-chain reconstruction score** of the frontier models tested in historical benchmarks. This measures recovery of what led to what; it does not establish general forecast accuracy or superiority to every frontier model. Full methodology and independent verification have not been supplied. Do not expand this into broader performance claims.

Independent research on Insights provides context, not an evaluation of Afterflow. Retain primary-source links and their limitations. Differentiate from general AI assistants through the connected simulation, delivery and measurement workflow, without claiming those tools cannot use company context.

## Brand and Content Principles

Use concise, concrete copy. Lead with the decision, show implementation, then explain how outcomes inform the next forecast. Keep assumptions, forecasts and observed results distinct. Avoid invented results, endorsements, integrations or compliance promises.

The visual system is white with black text, expressive Solare headings, Novela reading text and selective bronze emphasis; see [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md). “Book a demo” is the primary action and retains the supplied Calendly destination. Primary navigation contains Product, Research and Company. Research links to `/insights/`; the Trust Centre link is hidden until its content is ready.

The homepage sequence is hero → standalone introduction → three process chapters → audience → four-stage engine → company vision → team experience → research and governance → FAQs → final CTA → footer. Preserve full-height chapter framing, ordinary slow scrolling and the concise five-plus-six FAQ disclosure structure.

## Accessibility and Inclusion

Keep keyboard access, visible focus, reduced-motion support and meaningful mobile reading order. Essential copy, FAQs and the complete engine sequence remain available without JavaScript. Animated letters must not fragment accessible headings.
