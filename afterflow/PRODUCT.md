# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

AI transformation teams evaluating initiatives, rollout plans, and operating-model changes before committing company resources. The landing page also needs to make the product legible to the senior operators and decision-makers sponsoring that work.

## Product Purpose

Afterflow is a decision and simulation layer for operational change. It helps companies decide which AI initiatives are worth pursuing, design them properly, predict what happens at scale, and learn from observed outcomes after deployment.

## Positioning

Afterflow is a sandbox for operational decisions. It turns fragmented company data into a living model of the organisation, simulates proposed changes across teams, operations, customers, and measurable outcomes before commitment, then improves the model with rollout evidence.

## Operating Context

The product sits between transformation planning and deployment. Its working material includes fragmented operational data, organisation structures, processes, customer signals, proposed initiatives, rollout scenarios, predicted outcomes, and post-deployment observations. The simulation machinery stays behind the interface; visitors should understand what they can test and learn without needing to understand how it is computed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Build a living world model from fragmented company data.
- Test a proposed change against how the organisation actually works.
- Account for measurable constraints and the parts of work that depend on judgement.
- Compare possible futures across teams, operations, customers, and outcomes before a commitment is made.
- Incorporate observed deployment results so later decisions improve.
- Keep the website as a single scrollable landing page with a compact insights/blog presence.
- Do not present synthetic demonstration data as a customer result or validated benchmark.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Afterflow.
- Use the supplied Afterflow logo and favicon assets in `public/`.
- Use the supplied Strawford type family.
- The visual world is black, luxurious, and glass-like.
- Use Vengeance UI as the only component source; custom product-specific composition is allowed around those components.
- Clarity and compression should match Tenera; wording should have the directness of Arga Labs; the simulation should make the organisation feel alive in the spirit of Munder Difflin's office.
- Copy should be concise, clear, and concrete. Avoid inflated or load-bearing claims.
- The landing experience should follow the user-approved Recon composition: one cinematic full-bleed world, visible scroll depth, sparse translucent modules, a restrained cyan signal, and long intervals of deliberate negative space. Recon's assets, interface, and wording are reference material only and must not be copied.
- Do not lead with a simulated dashboard or office UI. Express the product through authored world-model imagery, causal diagrams, and concrete transformation questions.

## Product Principles

- Show the simulation mechanism before explaining it.
- Tie every scenario to teams, operations, customers, and outcomes.
- Make assumptions, predictions, and observed results distinguishable.
- Prefer one memorable demonstration over repeated feature claims.
- Keep evidence and uncertainty visible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The marketing site must remain keyboard accessible, preserve readable contrast, respect reduced-motion preferences, and adapt the simulation to narrow screens without hiding essential meaning.
